Drop a video here, or choose one.

How to film it so the numbers mean something
  • Front on, camera at hip height, roughly three metres back. Full body in frame with headroom.
  • Shoot at 60fps or higher. At 30fps a single turn is about fifteen frames, which is not enough to see the axis move.
  • Plain wall behind, even light, no other dancers in shot.
  • Start recording in the preparation and keep rolling two seconds past the landing.
  • Fitted clothing. Loose skirts and baggy tops confuse the hip and shoulder points.
